BEHIND THE SHAPEThe same condition, with a different point fixed.+

For attacker A, defender D and goal centre G, a shot is blocked exactly when

‖D − A‖ ≤ r   and   (D − A) · (G − A) ≥ ‖D − A‖ ‖G − A‖ cos(α/2).

Fix D and vary A for the inverse region; fix A and vary D for the usual circular sector. Boundaries are included and the region is clipped to the pitch. There is no extra requirement that the defender be closer to the goal than the attacker.

The sector includes its apex A = D. At A = G the aiming direction is undefined, so that attacker position is excluded. Shading and area are numerically sampled; the test-shot verdict uses the inequality directly.

FIELD / THEORY A point-player model. No ball speed, player size or goal width in the blocking rule.
